One decision before I build

Should privacy notes change the score?

Today the score only drops for security problems. Data leaving your home is scored only when the code shows your private data actually being sent. Telemetry is different, and your HomGar shows why. Its telemetry.py can send a daily ping to homgar-telemetry-worker.funkypeople.workers.dev, a server the developer runs. Reading the code, the ping contains a random ID, the HomGar and HA versions, and (only if you agree) your device models. It is off unless you switch it on. A scanner can see that the ping exists, but it can't reliably tell an opt-in ping from one that's always on, or work out what's in it.

  • My recommendation: show privacy notes prominently, with a purple chip on the card and their own section on the detail page, but don't score them. Scoring would cost HomGar points for doing telemetry the careful way. Proven theft of private data keeps its existing critical score.
  • The other way: a small deduction (low, −2) for each privacy note, so the cards reflect them too.

In short

Bambu Lab connects Home Assistant to your 3D printer, both directly on your home network and through Bambu Lab's cloud. Its dashboard card takes your Home Assistant login so it can load print history and camera video from your own Home Assistant. Every time we saw it do that, the login went to your Home Assistant and nowhere else. We found no path that sends your data to the internet.

What we found (2)
Worth a look

Its dashboard card uses your Home Assistant login

What it means
Dashboard cards run inside your Home Assistant page in the browser. This one reads your login token, the key your browser uses to prove it's you. Anything holding that key can do whatever you can do in Home Assistant.
Should you worry?
Probably not. All 5 places send it only to Bambu Lab's own pages on your Home Assistant (/api/bambu_lab/…), which is how cards are meant to load data. If it were ever sent to an outside site, this would turn red.
